## Description

Lexiguessr is a web-based word-guessing game that gives players dictionary and thesaurus hints to help them guess a hidden word while improving their vocabulary.

## Deployed Website Link

https://johnmarhsifs-cir.github.io/Lexiguessr/

## How to Access & Use

1. Visit https://johnmarhsifs-cir.github.io/Lexiguessr/ in your browser (or clone the repository and open `index.html`).
2. Select a difficulty level (`Easy` to `Extremely Hard`, from 5 to 12 letters).
3. Type a letter or word into the input box and click **Guess**.
4. Click **Hint** to reveal up to 10 progressive clues.
5. Guess the word before losing all **6 lives** to increase your score and streak.

## APIs Used

- **Random Word API** - Retrieves 5 random words based on selected difficulty and word length.
- **Merriam-Webster Collegiate & Thesaurus APIs** - Retrieves the word's definition, part of speech, syllables, synonyms, and antonyms to build the game hints.
